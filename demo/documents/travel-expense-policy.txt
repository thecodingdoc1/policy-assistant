BLUEFIELD TRANSPORT SERVICES
TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
Policy number: FIN-305. Version 1.

SECTION 1. SCOPE
This policy covers business travel and out-of-pocket business expenses for all employees. Travel must have a business purpose and be approved in advance by the traveler's manager.

SECTION 2. BOOKING AIR TRAVEL
Flights must be booked through the company travel portal at least 14 days before departure whenever possible. Flights booked later need a short explanation in the trip request. Travelers fly economy class on flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed only for flights of 6 hours or more, and only with Vice President approval.

SECTION 3. LODGING AND RENTAL CARS
Travelers stay at hotels listed in the travel portal when one is available near the work location. Rental cars should be compact or intermediate size. On domestic rentals, decline the rental company's collision insurance, because the company's own policy covers rental cars.

SECTION 4. MEALS AND PER DIEM
Meals are paid as a daily per diem instead of by receipt: $65 per day for domestic travel and $90 per day for international travel. On the first and last day of a trip the traveler receives 75 percent of the daily rate. Meals provided at a conference or by a client are deducted from the per diem. Alcohol is never reimbursed.

SECTION 5. PERSONAL VEHICLE MILEAGE
Employees who use their own vehicle for business are reimbursed at the company mileage rate of $0.67 per mile. Normal commuting between home and the regular work location is not reimbursable.

SECTION 6. RECEIPTS
An itemized receipt is required for every expense over $75, except per diem meals. Missing receipts must be replaced with a signed missing-receipt form, which the manager must approve.

SECTION 7. EXPENSE REPORTS
Submit the expense report within 30 days after the trip ends. Reports submitted more than 60 days after the trip ends are not reimbursed. The manager approves the report; Finance may reject expenses that do not follow this policy.
